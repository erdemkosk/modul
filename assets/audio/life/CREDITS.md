# Town life sounds

Every source is **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** on BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin):
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. No attribution is required; the author is credited
anyway. Cut, faded and loudness-normalised (mono 16-bit WAV, 22.05 kHz; the steam 44.1 kHz); the cut files
are CC0 as well.
