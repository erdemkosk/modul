required; they may not be redistributed, sold or shared on their own as sound effects or sound

distributed by Sonniss under the same royalty-free terms. Keep this repository private: the processed
files below are the game's sounds, not a sound pack to hand out. The vendors are credited here anyway.

file) holds every cut time, filter and level; run it on the folder of recordings to rebuild them.
