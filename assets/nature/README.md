# Nature set (trees, undergrowth, rocks)

Vegetation and rocks for the northern-forest / mountain world. Everything is generated by
`tools/blender/build_nature.py` (Blender 5.2, procedural bpy, fixed seeds, no image textures, no alpha):
foliage is real geometry (tapered spruce branch "roofs" with hanging branchlet fringes, clustered needle
clumps, small leaf cards), rocks are plane-cut, noise-displaced icospheres.

## Rebuild

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --python tools/blender/build_nature.py
```

Options after `--`: `--no-render` (export only, ~4 s), `--only spruce_a,rock_a` (rebuild a subset; the
other entries in `nature.json` are kept) and `--views trees,forest,...` (subset of previews). The full
run with all 8 Eevee previews takes about 30 s.

## Files

- `<id>.glb`: two meshes, `LOD0` (detailed) and `LOD1` (far, for beyond ~120 m, same silhouette and
  colours). Both have their origin at the trunk base / ground contact point and identity transforms.
  The scale is 1 unit = 1 m, and there are no armature, camera or light nodes.
- `nature.json`: per model `kind`, `height`, collision hint and `tris` [LOD0, LOD1], in Godot axes and metres.
  - Trees: `trunk_radius` (at 1 m height, for the winch / cylinder collider), `crown_radius` and
    `crown_base`.
  - Rocks / cliff: `size` and `center` of a box collider (85 % of the horizontal footprint).
  - Stump / log: cylinder `radius`, `center` and, for the log, `length` and `axis`.
- `previews/`: `trees`, `trees_lod1`, `small`, `rocks`, `props`, `spruce_closeup`, `birch_closeup`, `forest`.

| `fern` | fern clump | 0.67 m |
| `rock_a/b/c` | mossy boulders, c is a flat slab (≈15–20 % sunk below z = 0 for slopes) | 1.3 / 2.0 / 0.7 m |
| `cliff_a` | rock outcrop, footprint ≈ 7.4 × 5.7 m | 4.75 m |

Tree trunks and rock bottoms extend about 0.3 m below y = 0, so they can sit on slopes without
floating.

## Materials and colour

Material names: `Bark`, `BirchBark`, `DeadWood`, `Needles`, `Leaves`, `LeavesAutumn`, `Rock`, `Moss`,
`FernLeaf`, `Wood`. Each is a plain Principled material with the real base colour. The foliage
materials (`Needles`, `Leaves`, `LeavesAutumn`, `FernLeaf`) are double-sided.

Every mesh has `COLOR_0` (RGB ≤ 1), which multiplies the material colour. It is used for:

- fake AO: crown interiors and the undersides of clumps are darker;
- per-branch and per-clump tone;
- bark tint: `Bark` is the orange Scots pine colour, and spruce, stumps and logs tint it darker
  grey-brown;
- birch marks, rock creases and lichen.

Godot's importer turns on vertex-colour albedo by itself. A custom shader should multiply by `COLOR`.

Foliage has custom normals bent outward from the crown, so it shades softly as a volume. Keep them
when you import (the default). If a custom shader is used, read `NORMAL` from the mesh.
